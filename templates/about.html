The studio

Small by design.
Ambitious by nature.

Mock Interactive is an independent game and interactive studio built around a simple belief: memorable worlds are made deliberately.

II

We care about the things players feel before they can name them.

The weight of a menu. The silence before a reveal. A landmark on the horizon that makes you wonder what is beyond it. Those details are not decoration—they are the experience.

We combine worldbuilding, systems thinking, visual design, and development under one roof so every part speaks the same language.

01

Atmosphere is a system

Art, sound, pacing, interface, and mechanics should reinforce one another.

02

Clarity earns trust

Players should understand their choices without losing the joy of discovery.

03

Details carry the world

Wear, texture, language, and motion turn a designed space into a believable place.
